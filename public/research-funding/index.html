Application Pathway: how a member can apply

Institute membership on its own never satisfies a funder's university, nonprofit, geographic or professional requirement. Those conditions are listed separately on each entry. The Institute's own eligibility as an applicant organization has not yet been confirmed with any funder.

  • Independent Apply directly, or through your own practice where the funder accepts for-profit organizations. Other conditions may still apply.
  • Independent / AOI Your practice can be the applicant, or the Institute could apply or administer the award if its eligibility is confirmed.
  • AOI Affiliation Suggested You usually cannot apply alone. A qualifying nonprofit, institution or sponsor is needed; the Institute is one possible option.
  • Eligibility Unconfirmed A lead that needs one question answered before you invest time. The eligibility note says which question.

    About this directory

    What “Open” means

    Open means the application window was verified open on the date shown. It is never inferred from a recurring deadline. A program whose next cycle has not been published is shown as such, not as open. Confirm the official page before relying on any status; several windows close within weeks of the check date.

    Amounts and currencies

    Amounts are as the funder publishes them. “Not published” means the funder states no amount; nothing was estimated. Currencies are shown as published and not converted.

    Other research support

    Prizes for completed work, travel awards, crowdfunding platforms, in-kind credits, fee-based services such as IRB review, loan repayment and fellowships are useful but are not cash research grants. They are listed on their own tab so they are never mistaken for one.

    Small grants are rare

    Small cash grants that a practitioner can apply for without an institution are rare. That is the central finding of the research behind this directory, not a gap in it.
